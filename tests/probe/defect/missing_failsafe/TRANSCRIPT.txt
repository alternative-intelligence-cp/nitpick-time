FINDING. Across three compiler pins -- 94874ce, then 1.5.2's three commits to
0dfddac -- every diagnostic here is CHARACTER-IDENTICAL except for the line

headers to the three files (TM-115), not because the compiler moved. `case1`
is 30:1 in Part A0 and 62:1 in Part A; `case3` is 27:1 and 58:1. The counts,
the identity names, the order and the wording are the same text. DEF-5 is
stable.

"probably unchanged" is the assumption the whole re-pin discipline exists to
refuse. It cost four commands to find out, and it also caught the two
`expect-error-at:` line numbers, which WOULD have been wrong had they been
copied from Part A0.

THE COUNTS, AND THE CORRECTION THAT KEEPS BEING NEEDED. `case1` owes FOUR
identities -- Unreachable, HeapOom, HeapBadRequest, WildLeak -- because it has
no import, no arithmetic and no allocation, so its bill is `SAFETY.md` S-4b's
unconditional floor. A board carried SIX for it, relayed from the compiler
session. The six is real and belongs to `case3`, which imports the raiser: it
adds `probe11_arms_lib.EProbeZone` and `IntOverflow` to the floor. Both
numbers below were read out of the compiler's own output at each pin, never
predicted, and the stale `.ll` files were deleted before every run so that no
artifact of an older pin could be read as current.

NOTE ON `case3`'s OWN HEADER: the text it kept from 2026-09-03 says the true
cause is "you have no `failsafe`, and here are the NINE arms it owes". Nine
was never measured -- it is the arm count of `probe11_failsafe_arms.npk`, a
different program. The measured bill for `case3` is six. The sentence is left
as written, because it is what was believed then; this note and the file's own
new header are the correction.
