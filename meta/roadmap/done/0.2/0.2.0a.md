## Why `0.2.0a`, and why its own subcycle

**The adoption is its own subcycle, named `0.2.0a`, and it runs FIRST in cycle 0.2.** A letter marks an
inserted subcycle so that no existing number moves — this repository's 0.1.0b and 0.1.4c, `nitpick-regex`'s
0.1.0b, the compiler's 1.0.9a and 1.5.8b — and the cycle README's table is the order, as it was when 0.1.4c
ran before 0.1.3c. **`a`, not `b`**, because every lettered subcycle so far ran after its base and this one
runs before 0.2.0, whose name `0.2.0.md` is cited by the archived cycle-0.1 record.

- *Declined — `0.2.0b`:* reads as after 0.2.0, as 0.1.0b did.
- *Declined — `0.1.5b` or `0.1.6`:* cycle 0.1 is closed, audited and verified at `c8d4e8f`, and its records are
  archived; a subcycle there reopens a verified cycle.
- *Declined — renumbering (the adoption `0.2.0`, `Instant` `0.2.1`, …):* every number the cycle README, the
  roadmap, `CLAUDE.md` and the archived `0.1.5.md` cite would move, and an archived record is not edited.
- *Declined — inside 0.2.0:* an adoption and a feature in one subcycle are reviewed as neither (0.1.4c's
  reason), and 0.2.0's rehearsal needs this subcycle's tree as its base.
