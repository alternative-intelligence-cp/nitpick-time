================================================================
WHAT THIS SHOWS, AND WHAT IT DOES NOT
================================================================

SHOWN, here, at 300 rows, re-derivable in a seventh of a second:

  - a module-level `pub fixed` array of structs is emitted as an LLVM
    `constant`, never a mutable `global`;
  - the module carries no llvm.global_ctors, no llvm.global_dtors and
    no `appending` global of any kind, so no startup routine fills it;
  - the object places it in `.rodata` (flags `A`, no `W`) and the
    linked program maps it into a read-only LOAD segment;
  - the symbol's size is 300 x 16 bytes exactly, so `#size_of` and the
    linker agree about the row;
  - the executable has no .init_array section at all.

That is exactly SAFETY.md S-19's claim — `fixed` module state is
read-only memory, with no initialisation at startup, nothing to leak
and nothing to race — and it is ZONE_MODEL.md Z-7 and Z-8's premise.

NOT SHOWN here, and deliberately: that thirty thousand rows behave the
same way.  That was observed once, during the original probe 04 run on
2026-09-03, and re-deriving it costs 281 s and 30.9 GiB of RSS.  It is
recorded in meta/roadmap/done/0.0/0.0.0.md as a ONE-TIME OBSERVATION on that
basis.  The reason to believe it anyway is not the observation but the
mechanism: the emission form is chosen by the same lowering path for
any element count, and O-N4 is a cost defect, not a semantics one.
When O-N4 closes, run probe04_big_fixed_table.npk and append its own
transcript here.
