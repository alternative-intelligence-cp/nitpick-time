# `meta/research/`

Measurements a decision cites, and notes on the standards. A decision that
cites a measurement keeps the measurement here — the tzdb size survey behind
TM-007 is the first.

- [`CURRENCY.md`](CURRENCY.md) — every fact from outside the compiler that the
  plan rests on, with the version pinned and the date it was last checked.
  A cycle whose rows are unchecked is not ready to start.
- [`tzdb-size.md`](tzdb-size.md) — the compiled tzdb's size, TM-007 and TM-135.
- [`hinnant-date-algorithms.md`](hinnant-date-algorithms.md) — the source of
  `date_to_days` and `days_to_date` (TM-016), as read on 2026-09-25.
