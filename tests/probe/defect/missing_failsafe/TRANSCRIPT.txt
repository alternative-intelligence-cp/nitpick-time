repository made, granted in full.

"six identities" for `case1`, relayed from the compiler session. It is FOUR.
`case1` has no import, no arithmetic and no allocation, so its bill is
S-4b's unconditional floor: Unreachable, HeapOom, HeapBadRequest, WildLeak.

subtracts -- so it owes `probe11_arms_lib.EProbeZone` and `IntOverflow` on

output, not predicted; the instrument was commissioned first, and the stale
`.ll` files from 2026-09-03 were deleted before the run so that no artifact
of the old pin could be read as current.

`failsafe`, and here are the NINE arms it owes". Nine was never measured --
it is the arm count of `probe11_failsafe_arms.npk`, a different program. The
measured bill for `case3` is six. The header is left as written, because it
